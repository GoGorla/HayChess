About HayChess

HayChess is meant to be a free chess teaching website that also lets you play against other people. Chess is one of the best games ever made, and the tools for learning it shouldn't sit behind a subscription.

The Name

Hay (հայ) is the Armenian word for an Armenian person, and Armenians call their country Hayastan. The creator of this site is Armenian, so the name is a nod to that heritage. Armenia also has a proud chess culture. It is the home of world champion Tigran Petrosian, and chess is taught in Armenian schools as a regular subject.

Our Mission

Make chess easy to start and rewarding to keep learning. Beginners should be able to learn how the pieces move, then see those ideas used in real tactics and endgames. Experienced players should have a clean place to play and analyze.

Teach

Lessons and puzzles that build from the basics, kept free for everyone.

Play

Live public matches, plus private games with a join code for friends.

Analyze

A free board for working out ideas by yourself, with highlights and arrows.

What You Can Do Today

• Create or join public and private matches

• Play with full chess rules: castling, en passant, promotion, check, checkmate and stalemate

• Use premoves when it's your opponent's turn

• Right-click to highlight squares and draw orange arrows

• Explore positions on the analysis board

What's Coming

The Learn tab will fill with lessons and puzzles, and the board will get new themes and colors. Clocks, game history and accounts are on the wish list too.
